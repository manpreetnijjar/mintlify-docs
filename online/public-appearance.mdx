### Public Behavior Affects Security  
Hackers gather info from:
- Overheard conversations  
- Social media posts  
- Company badges  
- Screens visible in public  
- Conference chats  

## Do’s & Don’ts
<Cards>
  <Card title="Do">Stay professional, protect your screen, keep badges hidden.</Card>
  <Card title="Don’t">Discuss internal projects publicly or overshare on social media.</Card>
</Cards>
