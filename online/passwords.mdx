## The 4 Rules of Strong Passwords
1. Use 12+ characters  
2. Mix numbers, symbols, uppercase, lowercase  
3. Avoid personal details  
4. Never reuse passwords  

## Why MFA Matters  
Even if a password is stolen, hackers **cannot** get in — they need your phone, code, or token.
