---
title: Safe Internet & Device Usage
---

Best practices for using the internet and devices securely to protect organizational data and systems.
---


Safe internet and device usage are essential to preventing cyber threats such as malware, data theft, and unauthorized access. Employees play a key role in protecting organizational systems by following secure browsing and device handling practices.

Security awareness applies to all devices and networks, both inside and outside the workplace.

---

## Why It Matters?

Unsafe internet or device use can lead to:

- Malware and ransomware infections 
- Data leaks and unauthorized access 
- Credential theft and account compromise 
- Network intrusion through infected devices 

Many cyber incidents start with unsafe browsing or unsecured devices.

---

## Internet Usage Risks

- Visiting untrusted or malicious websites 
- Clicking unknown or suspicious links 
- Downloading unauthorized software or files 
- Using unsecured public Wi-Fi networks 
- Ignoring browser security warnings 

---

## Device Usage Risks

- Leaving devices unlocked or unattended 
- Using outdated software or operating systems 
- Connecting unknown USB drives or accessories 
- Disabling security controls or updates 
- Mixing personal and work use without approval 

---

## Safe Internet Practices

### Do

- Use approved browsers and security tools 
- Verify website URLs before entering information 
- Download software only from trusted sources 
- Keep browsers and plugins up to date 
- Use a VPN when required, especially on public Wi-Fi 

### Don’t

- Click pop-ups or suspicious ads 
- Download pirated or unauthorized software 
- Ignore security warnings from browsers or systems 
- Access sensitive data on unsecured networks 
- Disable security protections 

---

## Safe Device Practices

### Do's

- Lock devices when not in use 
- Keep operating systems and applications updated 
- Use strong passwords and MFA 
- Report lost or stolen devices immediately 
- Follow company device usage policies 

### Don’t

- Share devices or login credentials 
- Connect unknown USB drives 
- Jailbreak or root devices 
- Store sensitive data on unsecured devices 
- Bypass security controls 

---

## Real-World Example

An employee connects a personal USB drive to a work computer. 
The drive contains malware that spreads across the network.

➡️ A simple action causes a major security incident.

---

## Key Takeaway

Safe internet and device usage protect both you and the organization.

> **If a device or website doesn’t seem safe, don’t use it.**
