# Introduction to Cybersecurity

## Why it matters  
Your actions directly impact the safety of our company, customers, and data. Most breaches start with **human error**, not a hacker.

## Key Concepts
<Cards>
  <Card title="Confidentiality">Keep information private.</Card>
  <Card title="Integrity">Ensure data is not changed incorrectly.</Card>
  <Card title="Availability">Systems are accessible when needed.</Card>
</Cards>
