# What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of **protecting systems, networks, and data** from digital attacks, unauthorized access, and damage.

---

## Why Cybersecurity Matters?

- Protects **personal and company data**

- Prevents **financial loss** and identity theft

- Reduces risk of **data breaches and ransomware**

- Ensures **business continuity** and trust

- Supports **legal and compliance requirements**

---

## Common Cyber Threats:

- Phishing and social engineering  

- Malware and ransomware  

- Password attacks  

- Insider threats  

- Unsecured networks and devices  

---

## Core Cybersecurity Principles

- **Confidentiality:** Data is only accessible to authorized users  

- **Integrity:** Data is accurate and not altered improperly  

- **Availability:** Systems and data are accessible when needed  

---

## Basic Security Best Practices

- Use **strong passwords** and **MFA**

- Keep systems and software **updated**

- Be cautious with **emails, links, and attachments**

- Follow company **security policies**

- Report suspicious activity immediately

---
Cybersecurity is **everyone’s responsibility**—technology and people must work together to stay secure.
